About

Research with an eye toward implementation.

My work sits at the intersection of healthcare, technology, and operational improvement. I am interested in understanding where software and processes create friction—and how informatics can help organizations design better ways of working.

As a doctoral student in Health Informatics at Rutgers University, I am developing a research portfolio focused on practical improvements that can translate into real-world healthcare settings.
